Data deletion

Delete Your TechFlow Data

Last updated: 31 July 2026

TechFlow has no user account system. Operational business data is stored locally on your device rather than in a TechFlow cloud account.

Data covered by these instructions

Depending on how you use TechFlow, locally stored data may include:

  • Clients and their contact details.
  • Quotations, invoices, payments and reports.
  • Interventions, reminders and calendar-related information.
  • Inventory items and quantities.
  • Company profile information, tax settings, templates and app preferences.
  • Logos, attachments or files that you deliberately add to the app.
  • Exported backup files that you create and save outside the app.

How to delete your data

Delete individual records

Open the relevant section in TechFlow and use the available delete action for the client, quotation, invoice, intervention, inventory item, report or other record.

Delete all local app data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → TechFlow → Storage, then choose Clear storage or Clear data. Menu names may vary by device manufacturer.

Uninstall TechFlow

Uninstalling TechFlow removes the app and its private local application data from that device.

Delete exported backups

Backup files are stored wherever you chose to save or share them. They are not automatically removed when you clear the app or uninstall it. Delete those files manually from your device, cloud drive, email, messaging app or other storage location.

Important: Current TechFlow backup files are not encrypted. A SHA-256 checksum checks integrity but does not hide their contents. Delete every copied backup if you no longer want that data retained.

Data retained by TechFlow

TechFlow does not operate cloud synchronization for your clients, invoices, quotations, interventions, inventory or backup contents. Therefore, TechFlow normally has no remote copy of that business data to delete.

If an optional purchase-verification service is enabled, it may retain limited purchase-verification and security-log information for fraud prevention, troubleshooting and legal compliance. It does not intentionally receive your operational business records.

Data retained by Google Play or other services

Google Play may retain purchase, subscription, transaction, account, device and fraud-prevention records according to Google's own policies and applicable law. TechFlow cannot delete records held by Google.

Data that you deliberately share with WhatsApp, email providers, cloud drives, calendars or other third-party apps must be deleted through those services.

Request assistance

No request is required to delete locally stored TechFlow data. You can use the steps above at any time.

For questions about limited data that may be held by a TechFlow-operated verification service, contact:

Include “TechFlow data deletion request” in the subject line and describe the data or service involved. Do not email passwords, full payment-card details, purchase tokens or sensitive business records.

Retention period

  • Local business data: retained on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app.
  • Exported backups: retained in the location you selected until you delete every copy.
  • Limited verification/security records: retained only as reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, legal or accounting obligations.
  • Google Play records: retained according to Google's policies and applicable law.
