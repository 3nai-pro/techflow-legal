Your business data stays under your control.

TechFlow is an offline-first field-service application. Business records are stored locally on your device. Limited network communication may occur for Google Play Billing, optional subscription verification, and user-initiated interactions with third-party apps.

At a glance

  • No advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics.
  • No TechFlow-operated cloud synchronization of clients, invoices, quotations, inventory, or interventions.
  • Google Play processes purchases and subscription status for Android distributions using Play Billing.
  • An optional secure verification service may receive limited purchase-verification data, but not your business records.
  • Backups are user-created JSON files with a SHA-256 integrity checksum; current backups are not encrypted.
Important: Exported backup files can be read by anyone who obtains them. Store them in a protected location and avoid sending them through insecure channels.
