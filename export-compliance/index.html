Compliance notice

Encryption and export controls

Last updated: 30 July 2026

TechFlow uses standard encryption and security functionality provided by Android, Google Play, HTTPS/TLS, Expo and open-source libraries. This includes secure local storage, cryptographic random identifiers, SHA-256 integrity checking and encrypted network transport where network communication is configured.

TechFlow does not implement a proprietary cryptographic algorithm and does not market encryption as its primary purpose. Backup files are currently not encrypted.

No legal classification is claimed on this page. Export classification and reporting obligations depend on the final distributed build, distribution method, developer/exporter circumstances and applicable U.S. and other laws. The publisher must complete the Google Play export declaration truthfully and obtain specialist advice where needed.

Distribution restrictions

Google Play may restrict downloads in embargoed or sanctioned locations. Users may not download, export, re-export or use TechFlow in violation of applicable export-control or sanctions laws.
