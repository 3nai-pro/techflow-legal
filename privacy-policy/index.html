Privacy Policy

TechFlow — Field Service Management

Effective date: 30 July 2026 · Last updated: 30 July 2026

Summary: TechFlow stores your operational business records locally. It does not operate cloud synchronization for those records. Google Play and, when configured, a secure subscription-verification service may process limited purchase and technical data needed for licensing and fraud prevention.

1. Who we are

TechFlow is an offline-first field-service management application for technicians and small service businesses. It is published by TechFlow, with correspondence in Orsay, France. Questions may be sent to techflowlb@outlook.com.

2. Data stored locally

TechFlow stores business records and configuration in the private application storage of your device. This may include clients, contact details entered or imported by you, interventions, invoices, quotations, payments, inventory, company profile information, rates, tax settings, document numbering, logos, templates, reminders and display preferences.

TechFlow does not operate a cloud service that synchronizes or remotely stores these business records.

4. Data TechFlow does not intentionally collect

  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.
  • Behavioral advertising or marketing profiles.
  • Continuous precise location.
  • Browsing history.
  • Business records for cloud synchronization.
  • Backup contents on TechFlow-operated servers.

TechFlow currently includes no advertising SDK and no behavioral analytics SDK. Platform providers and stores may independently process technical information according to their own terms.

5. Device permissions

Contacts

Contacts access is requested only when you choose to import a client. Imported details become a local client record. You may enter the details manually instead.

Calendar

Calendar access is requested only when you choose to add an intervention to the device calendar.

Notifications

Notification permission is used for local reminders scheduled on your device.

Files and media

The system picker or share interface is used for files you explicitly select, export or share. TechFlow does not scan unrelated files in the background.

6. Backups

Backups are created and restored only at your request. They contain a complete copy of relevant TechFlow business data in JSON format.

  • Integrity: A SHA-256 checksum helps detect corruption or modification.
  • Encryption: Current backup files are not encrypted. A checksum does not hide their contents.
  • Storage: You select where the file is saved or shared.
  • Responsibility: Protect the file with device security, an encrypted folder, or a trusted storage provider with appropriate access controls.
Do not treat a TechFlow backup as password-protected. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read its contents.

7. Security

TechFlow uses the application sandbox, standard platform cryptography, HTTPS for configured verification endpoints, secure device storage for entitlement cache information, cryptographically generated record identifiers and backup integrity checks. No system can guarantee absolute security. You remain responsible for device access controls and exported files.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear application data or uninstall TechFlow. Because TechFlow has no user account system and does not remotely store business records, it cannot remotely erase local records for you.

Google Play and any configured verification provider may retain purchase-related records for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention and service-operation purposes under their respective policies and applicable law.

9. Legal bases and rights

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, local processing is performed at your direction to provide the application. Limited purchase verification is necessary to provide paid functionality, prevent fraud and manage subscriptions. You can access, correct, export and delete local records inside the app. Requests concerning data held by Google should be directed to Google. For limited data held by a TechFlow verification service, contact us at the address below.

10. Children
