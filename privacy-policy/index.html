Privacy Policy

TechFlow — Field Service Management  ·  Effective 28 July 2026

Privacy at a glance: TechFlow is an offline-first application. It does not operate a backend server and does not automatically transmit your business records to the publisher.

1. Publisher and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the TechFlow mobile application (the “Application”). The Application is published by Naji Nassar, acting under the product name “TechFlow”, with correspondence location in Orsay, France. Contact: techflowlb@outlook.com.

This notice explains how the Application handles information on your device and how the publisher handles any information you send directly by email.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

TechFlow allows you to enter and manage business records such as client details, job records, quotations, invoices, payments, inventory, notes, settings, and business branding.

Structured application records are stored locally using on-device application storage. Files selected or generated by you, including logos, PDFs, spreadsheets, exports, backups, and temporary share files, may also be handled through the device file system and system document picker.

The publisher does not receive or remotely access these records.

3. Your Role When Entering Other People’s Data

When you enter personal data relating to clients, employees, suppliers, or other individuals, you decide why and how that information is used. You or your business will normally be the data controller for that information.

You are responsible for having a lawful basis, giving any required privacy notices, limiting access, applying appropriate retention periods, keeping the information accurate, and responding to data-subject requests.

4. Permissions and Device Features

Depending on the features you use, TechFlow may request access to contacts, calendar, notifications, files or documents, photos, camera, or sharing functions. Permissions are requested only when needed for the relevant feature and can be managed in your device settings.

Granting an operating-system permission is not the same as consenting to unrelated processing. TechFlow does not use permissions for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

5. Network Access and Third-Party Applications

TechFlow does not operate a backend server and does not automatically transmit your business records to us. Network access may occur when you deliberately open a legal webpage, email application, messaging application, map, telephone link, or another third-party service.

When you choose to share an invoice, quotation, backup, message, or other content, the selected data is transferred to the third-party application you choose. That third party processes the information under its own privacy terms.

6. Backups, Exports, and Temporary Files

Backup and export operations are user-initiated. Exported files are saved or shared to a location you select. They are not sent to the publisher.

Current backup files may not be encrypted. Anyone who obtains a copy may be able to read its contents. Store backups securely and delete obsolete copies.

Files stored outside the Application’s private storage, copies retained by third-party services, operating-system backups, and files shared to other applications are not removed automatically when you delete data inside TechFlow or uninstall the Application.

7. Retention and Deletion

Application records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the Application’s data, or uninstall it. Temporary generated files may remain for a limited period depending on the operating system and sharing workflow. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete them there.

8. Security

The operating system normally isolates the Application’s private storage from other applications. This protection may be reduced on rooted, jailbroken, compromised, debug-enabled, or otherwise insecure devices.

TechFlow uses cryptographically generated identifiers for records and integrity checks for supported backup workflows. These measures do not replace device security, secure backup storage, and appropriate access controls.

9. Information You Send to Support

If you contact support, the publisher may receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your request, diagnose issues, protect the Application, and retain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, payment-card data, or unnecessary client information.

10. Children

TechFlow is a professional business tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

11. Your Rights

For data stored only on your device, you can access, correct, export, or delete it directly in the Application. Because the publisher cannot remotely access that data, the publisher generally cannot alter or delete it for you.

Requests concerning client or employee data entered by a TechFlow user should normally be directed to the business or professional who entered that data.

For personal data received directly by the publisher, such as support correspondence, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law by emailing techflowlb@outlook.com. Where applicable, you may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In France, this is the Commission nationale de l’informatique et des libertés (CNIL).

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Application, legal requirements, or our practices. The revised policy will display a new effective date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing materially different processing.
