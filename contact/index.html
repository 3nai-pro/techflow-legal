Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Where is my business data stored?

TechFlow stores clients, quotations, invoices, interventions, inventory and settings locally on your device. TechFlow does not operate cloud synchronization for these business records in the current release.

How do I back up my data?

Use the Backup & Restore section in TechFlow Settings. Exported backups are JSON files with an integrity checksum, but they are not encrypted. Store them in a protected location.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play. Open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TechFlow.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Your existing records remain available for viewing and backup export. Actions that create or modify business data become restricted until access is restored.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete individual records in the app, clear TechFlow storage in Android Settings, uninstall the app, and manually delete exported backup files. See the Delete My Data page for full instructions.

Can TechFlow recover data after I uninstall the app?

No. TechFlow cannot remotely recover locally stored data. Keep an exported backup before uninstalling or clearing app storage.
